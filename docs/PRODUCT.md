# SecondBrain AI

## Vision

SecondBrain AI is an AI-powered knowledge fusion platform that helps students, researchers, and professionals build a reliable second brain by combining knowledge from multiple sources into one intelligent workspace.

Instead of simply answering questions, SecondBrain AI understands, connects, verifies, and organizes information with complete source traceability.

---

## Problem Statement

Students consume information from many different places:

- PDFs
- PPTs
- YouTube videos
- Handwritten notes
- Images
- Web articles

Existing AI tools summarize each source independently but fail to combine knowledge across multiple resources.

Users spend significant time switching between materials, verifying information, identifying contradictions, and filling missing concepts.

There is no unified platform that transforms fragmented learning resources into a connected knowledge system.

---

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current tools mainly focus on:

- Question answering
- Summarization
- Note generation

They rarely:

- connect multiple sources
- detect contradictions
- identify missing concepts
- explain where knowledge originated
- maintain complete source provenance

---

## Our Core Innovation

SecondBrain AI treats every uploaded resource as a knowledge source.

Instead of storing documents, it extracts meaningful knowledge claims, links related concepts together, preserves evidence, and builds an intelligent knowledge graph.

The platform focuses on knowledge understanding rather than simple document retrieval.

---

- University Students

- Researchers

## MVP

The first version will support:

## Future Vision

Future versions will include:

- Knowledge gap detection
- Contradiction detection
- Automatic revision planning
- Flashcards
- Quiz generation

- Team knowledge spaces
- Multi-agent reasoning
- Voice interaction

---

## Non-Goals

SecondBrain AI is NOT intended to become:

- Another ChatGPT clone
- A simple PDF summarizer
- A generic note-taking application

The primary goal is knowledge fusion and understanding.

---

## Success Metrics

The platform succeeds when users can:

- Learn faster
- Find information instantly
- Understand relationships between concepts
- Trust AI responses through evidence
- Reduce time spent searching across resources
